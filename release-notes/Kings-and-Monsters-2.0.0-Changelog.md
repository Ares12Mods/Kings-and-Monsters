# Kings & Monsters 2.0.0 (Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge)

I'm really excited about this one. It's been a long time coming and it's the biggest update the mod has had.

I redid every animation for the ogres from scratch, gave them new attacks, and smoothed out how they and the other mobs path and chase so fights feel a lot better. There are new voices and attack sounds too, plus a brand new boss track for the Ogre King made by Soul. On top of that there are new creatures, new structures, a tribute and quest system, and a lot more to explore. Everything below is new since 1.2.2.

Requires NeoForge 21.1.230+, GeckoLib 4.8+ and Curios 9+. Patchouli is now optional. Boss Checklist, JEI, Jade and Better Combat are optional too.

## The swamp
- **Bog Toad**: a big swamp predator that hops at you and bites with Bogrot II. Its landings daze whatever is under it. Toads are always hostile to players and can't be bred.
- **Bog Pole**: the toad's young. Harmless, flees to deep water, and grows up in about an hour (fish speed it up).
- **Bog Toad Eggs**: found on muddy banks. Near water they hatch into poles, which is the only way new toads appear.
- **Plague Ogre**: an Ogre Grunt that died infected with Bogrot II. They wander swamps and the edges of plains outposts, and the rest of the Tribe hunts them.
- **Bogrot**: slows movement and attack speed by 10%, deals damage that ignores armor, and lasts 45 seconds. Ogres take 15% more from it, the Ogre King 50% more, players half. Milk cures it.
- **Bogfang Blade**: infects with Bogrot, and three critical hits on an infected target rupture it for all the remaining damage. The new **Bogburst** enchantment spreads the rupture to nearby enemies.
- **Boghide armor**: made from smelted toad skin. Boots give faster swimming and a toad leap, and the full set makes you immune to Bogrot.
- **Toad food**: Raw and Cooked Toad Legs, and Toad Stew, which gives Ogre's Bane (+15% damage against ogres).
- **Potions**: Bogrot, Bog Sight and Amphibiousness.

## Mudsnout boars
- Wild boars no longer attack on sight. They keep their distance, and if you crowd one it turns, snorts, and gives you three seconds to back off before it charges. Hit one, or corner it, and it fights.
- Tame them with toad meat, apples or acorns (each boar needs 6 to 15 trust). Saddle, ride, and charge with them.
- Breed them for speed and health. Foals inherit their parents' traits.
- Mud Wallows (two variants) generate on the plains.
- Ogre Archers ride boars as part of the Tribe: at outposts, the fort, the quarry and on patrols.
- **Blackhide armor** from boar hide. The full set gives a running Charge that rams enemies.
- New food and gear: acorns, boar haunch, Haunch Platter, Bone Broth, Bone Club, Boar Calling Horn.

## The Ogre Tribe
- **Favor**: your own standing with the Tribe. At 50 favor you're appeased and ogres leave you alone.
- **Gold Coins**: hold one out to pass for ten minutes while the Tribe's anger is low, or give them to a Captain or the King for 5 favor each.
- **Quests**: 18 errands from Captains and the King, three to pick from at a time, with a tracker on screen and a favor bar on the quest screens. Once you're appeased, Captains talk to you without a coin.
- **The King's court**: wake him and he talks to you. Trade at 80 favor for his relics and a daily market. At full favor you can ask for a fair duel with no captains. He fights to a yield, you keep your favor, and Bog Iron opens up for the world. He remembers who beat him, rests for 10 minutes before the next duel, and goes back to his throne.
- **Ogre King's Crown**: calls one of four warbands to fight for you for 90 seconds, then rests for 5 minutes.
- **Merchant**: coin and diamond trades, a Fort Map on every merchant, the Ogre Truce, and rare stock for Golden Ogre Teeth.
- New ogre gear: Brute's Helm, Bogsteel Chestplate, Bogsteel Gauntlets, Ogre Loincloth, Ogre Stomach, Padded Earmuffs.

## The Ogre King
- A brand new boss track by Soul: an intro, a score for each phase, a transition into phase two and a proper ending, all timed to the fight.
- New boss bar, and Boss Checklist support.
- You can jump over his stomp ripples, and the Double Stomp hits for less.
- His roar now also slows and weakens your pets, tamed boars and summoned army.
- His club strikes and ripples now hit Plague Ogres. His ripples do half damage to the Tribe's own.
- He walks straight at you instead of zigzagging.
- Drops 12 to 20 gold nuggets, 2 to 4 gold coins and 2 to 3 Golden Ogre Teeth, on top of his usual loot.

## Ogres
- Every ogre has new animations, and most have new attacks. Archers swing as they walk, and Brutes, Captains and the King throw swings over their walk. Mages summon a line of jaws from the mire.
- **Ogre Trapper**: new hunter found at Trapper Huts and Boar Traps. His Trapper's Knife slows, hamstrings and makes you bleed.
- Every ogre now drops gold nuggets. Grunt Captains have 20 armor and the Guard's bite gives Regeneration III.
- Smoother pathing: Grunts, Captains, Guards, Trappers, Plague Ogres, Brutes, boars and toads now chase in a straight line across open ground instead of zigzagging.
- Ogres and their jockey boars stop attacking you if you pay the toll or become appeased mid-fight.

## World
- New structures: the King's Quarry, Supply Camps, a Moving Camp, an Abandoned Camp, Trapper Huts, Boar Traps and Mud Wallows, with their own loot and residents.
- Bog Toads, Bog Poles, Plague Ogres and Mudsnout boars now spawn naturally.
- Bog Iron generates in swamps, mangroves and lush caves. It stays locked until the King is beaten or yields a duel.

## Enchantments and items
- **Tyrant** now works on swords, axes, maces and the ogre relics, but not tridents or spears. Fully surrounded it adds up to +1.6, +2.75 and +3.75 damage at levels I to III, just above Sharpness V.
- **Heavy Throw** now works on tridents as well as the Hunter's Spear.
- New enchantments: **Timber** (fells whole trees) and **Brightwick** (brighter Candle Helmet).
- **Candle Helmet**: a candle you wear for light that follows you.
- Fresh meat stays fresh through cooking.

## Sound
- Every ogre now has its own idle, hurt and death sounds: Grunt, Captain, Archer, Brute, Mage, Guard, Trapper, Merchant, Plague Ogre and the King. Captains also have a salute.
- New attack sounds to go with the new moves: the Brute's jump and Temper Tantrum, the Mage's cast windup and release, and the King's pre-attack grunts, summon call and warning snort.
- Redone hurt sounds for the Grunt, Archer and Mage, and new roars, huff and hurt sounds for the King.
- Mudsnout boars have idle, hurt and death sounds (with their own set for the young), a charge burst and charge snorts. Bog Toads get deep, lowered croaks and hurt sounds.
- The new Ogre King boss track by Soul, with its own intro, two phases, transition and ending.
- More than 120 new sound files in all.

## Extras
- A full in-game guidebook (Patchouli) and Jade and JEI info for the new content.
- Optional compatibility with Spell Power, Iron's Spells and Wildscapes.
- New particles, and advancement tabs for boars, the swamp and the Tribe.

## Fixes
- Mud Wallow Variant 2 no longer leaves a ring of dirt around the spire.
- Fort jockeys spawn inside the walls and no longer suffocate under low ceilings.
- New config option for the duel rest time (`duelCooldownMinutes`).
