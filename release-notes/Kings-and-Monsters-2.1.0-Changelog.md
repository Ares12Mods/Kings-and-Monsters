# Kings & Monsters 2.1.0 (1.21.1 NeoForge)

- New Ogre King attack: the Butt Smash. He leaps straight up and comes crashing down on the same spot
- Everyone in the circle where he lands takes a crushing hit and is thrown clear. It hurts most right under him
- The landing sends out a ripple that reaches further than his Double Stomp. It hits harder up close and weakens toward the edge. Jump it to avoid it
- He is slow to get back up after landing. This is your chance to hit him
- He can use it in both phases of the fight, at most every 45 seconds in phase one and every 35 in phase two
- All of the Ogre King's ripples now weaken with distance, down to about 10 damage at the far end
- The Ogre King's club double overhead hits softer. It still hits harder than his single overhead, but less than the Butt Smash
- The Butt Smash knocks a raised shield down in its inner circle. Only the circle dazes, so being clipped by the ripple leaves you able to hit back
- The Ogre King's bare-handed double overhead slam does less damage and is used less often (30 second cooldown, was 20)
- The Strength and Resistance he gains when drawing his club now last 30 seconds (was 60)
- His club head thrust now has a 10 second cooldown
- Lower armor: Ogre King 20 (was 25), Ogre Brute 18 (was 20), Grunt Captain 15 (was 20). This applies to existing worlds too: custom armor values for these three need to be set again under `baseArmor`
- The Ogre Brute now kicks up a puff of dust when he takes off for his jump smash
