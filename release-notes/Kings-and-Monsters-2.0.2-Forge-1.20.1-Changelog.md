# Kings & Monsters 2.0.2 (Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge)

The first Forge 1.20.1 release. It is a port of the 1.21.1 NeoForge 2.0.2 version with the same content: the Ogre tribe, favor and quests, outposts, the Ogre King's fort, the Bog Toad swamp, Mudsnout Boars, bog iron gear and the compendium. This list covers what is different from the 1.21.1 version.

## Requirements
- Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge 47.4 or newer and Java 17.
- GeckoLib 4.8 or newer and Curios 5.14 or newer are required.
- Jade, JEI, Patchouli (for the compendium) and Better Combat are optional.

## What is different from the 1.21.1 version
- **Royal Trials:** 1.20.1 has no trial spawners, so the Ogre King's fort uses ordinary monster spawners. A trial begins when you come within 3 blocks of its spawner, and you win it by defeating its leaders (5 for the Mage trial, 5 for the Brute trial and 4 for the Captain trial). The King is still woken by talking to him.
- **1.21 content that doesn't exist in 1.20.1** is left out: the Mace and Breeze, Bogged and Armadillo mobs, and the Trial Chambers, along with the world generation that avoided them.
- **Enchantments, banner patterns and loot modifiers** are registered the Forge 1.20.1 way instead of through data components. In play they behave the same, and your items keep their enchantments, names and banner designs.
- **Weapon and armor stats** are applied exactly as in 1.21.1, even though 1.20.1 handles item attributes differently.
- **Better Combat:** the 1.20.1 build supports Better Combat 1.9 instead of 2.3, and it stays optional.
- **Ogre slam sounds:** without the Mace, ogre club impacts use softer sounds than the anvil clang.

## Same as 1.21.1 2.0.2
Everything in the 1.21.1 2.0.2 notes is in this release as well: the army fights the tribe and brawls with it, the favor tuning (5 favor per kill and the two-kill window), the Ogre Merchant's 40-minute stay with bodyguards that leave with him, the Bog Toad death sounds, the Ogreblood Totem roar, the "Complete Quest" button and the updated compendium favor page.
