# Kings & Monsters 2.0.2 (Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge)

A hotfix with some tuning for how the tribe's favor and your summoned army behave, plus a few quality-of-life fixes.

## Fixes
- Your summoned ogre army now actually fights the tribe's ogres. Before, the army would stand next to wild ogres without attacking them.
- The Ogre Merchant's bodyguards now leave with the merchant, and are cleaned up if the merchant can't be placed in the world.

## Favor and the tribe
- Killing an ogre now costs 5 favor. If you are at high favor, an accidental hit only costs a small fee and only the ogre you hit answers, not the whole tribe.
- Kill two ogres within five minutes and the whole tribe turns on you, like before.
- Once one of your summoned ogres attacks another ogre, it becomes a brawl: every ogre around the one you hit joins in against your army.
- If you are at high favor, your army keeps the peace and will not start fights with the tribe. It joins in if you attack an ogre, if the tribe attacks you or your army, or if your favor drops.

## Ogre Merchant
- A merchant now stays for 40 minutes, like the Wandering Trader.
- The merchant no longer turns invisible at night. He has bodyguards.

## Other changes
- Bog Toads have their own deep death sounds.
- The Ogreblood Totem now lets out one of the Ogre King's pre-attack roars when it saves you.
- The quest turn-in button now says "Complete Quest" instead of "Report Back".
- The compendium's favor page now explains what killing ogres costs.
