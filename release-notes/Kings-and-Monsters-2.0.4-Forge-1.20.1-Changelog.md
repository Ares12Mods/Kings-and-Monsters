# Kings & Monsters 2.0.4 (1.20.1 Forge)

- Fixed a crash on launch with Boss Checklist installed
- Fewer patrols in multiplayer: a group at one base now gets one patrol per cooldown, even after a server restart
- Robbing a camp as a group now sends one retaliation patrol instead of one per player
- Fixed updated worlds keeping the old 10-15 minute patrol cooldown. It is now 40-60 minutes (custom values need to be set again under `patrolMinCooldownTicks` and `patrolMaxCooldownTicks`)
- Added new King quests for Elder Guardians, Evokers and Illusioners
- Buffed the Hunter's Spear to 10 attack damage, 1.2 attack speed and 10 thrown damage. Its tooltip now shows the cripple chance
- Ogre Guards now only cause bleeding with their spear. Their bite cripples and their slam does neither
- Boars now float lower, swim a bit faster and can climb out of water
- Ridden boars now bob while swimming and can no longer charge in deep water
- You can now headbutt with a boar while holding a weapon, and still throw your spear
- Wild boars and Tribe Ogres now leave each other alone
- Your warband no longer turns on your pets, boars or summons
- Badly hurting an ogre while appeased now makes it fight to the death
- Fixed ogres walking while caught in a Bogjaw Trap
- Bogjaw Traps now catch neutral mobs and piglins. Tribe traps no longer catch ogres, and Trappers never catch themselves
- Trappers you fight can now trap you even if appeased, and your warband's Trappers set traps that work for you
- Bleeding tooltips and compendium entries now say hits apply Bleeding
