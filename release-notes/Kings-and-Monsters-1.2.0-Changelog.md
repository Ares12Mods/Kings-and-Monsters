# Kings & Monsters v1.2.0

For Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge.

## Enchantments

- Fixed Bog Iron armor, tools, and weapons not accepting their appropriate vanilla enchantments.
- The full Bog Iron equipment set now supports the same vanilla enchantment categories as its diamond equivalents.
- Fixed the Ogre King's Crown so it accepts the appropriate helmet enchantments.
- Added carefully limited vanilla enchantment pools to the Hunter's Spear, Iron Maw, Ogre King's Club, and Ogre Hookblade.
- Restricted Barbed and Heavy Throw to the Hunter's Spear.
- Restricted Tyrant to the Iron Maw and Ogre King's Club.
- The Ogre King's Club now receives Tyrant's intended combat bonus.
- Added Enchantment Descriptions compatibility for Barbed, Heavy Throw, and Tyrant.

## Weapon Improvements

- Sweeping Edge now upgrades the Ogre Hookblade's pull, hooking every enemy damaged by the same sweep.
- Updated the Hookblade tooltip to explain the Sweeping Edge interaction without making it look pre-enchanted.
- Fixed the Hunter's Spear changing to the wrong inventory/hotbar icon while charging a throw.

## Notes

- Custom enchantments cannot be applied to vanilla weapons or unsupported Kings & Monsters weapons.
- Existing weapon stats, Bog Iron balance, mob AI, combat behavior, and progression remain unchanged.
