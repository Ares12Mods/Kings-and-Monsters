# Kings & Monsters 2.0.3 (Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge)

A small update with new Ogre King boss bar art.

## Changes
- The Ogre King has a new boss bar with his name worked into the artwork.
- The Jade overlay no longer shows over the Ogre King, since he now has his own boss bar. If you want it back, set `showJadeForOgreKing` to `true` in `kingsandmonsters-client.toml`.
