# Kings & Monsters

A NeoForge 1.21.1 adventure mod. The Ogre Tribe holds outposts across the land, answers to a Fort-dwelling Ogre King, and remembers how you treat it. Beat its warriors for relics and bog iron, the swamp-forged metal behind a full set of gear.

## What it adds

- **The Ogre Tribe:** animated Grunts, Captains, Archers, Mages, Brutes and Guards, each with their own attacks and behavior, plus a travelling Ogre Merchant.
- **Outposts and the Fort:** ogre outposts spawn across the world, and the Ogre King's Fort holds the royal encounter. Tribute chests, a faction anger system and patrols respond to how you act.
- **The Ogre King:** a multi-phase boss with his own music, roars, slams and royal defenses.
- **Bog iron:** a swamp-forged metal and a full set of tools and armor made from it.
- **Relics and artifacts:** Curios items taken from the Tribe, plus new enchantments.
- **An in-game guidebook:** the Kings & Monsters compendium, built with Patchouli.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.230 or newer
- GeckoLib 4.8 or newer
- Curios 9.0 or newer
- Patchouli 1.21.1-93 or newer

Optional: Better Combat, JEI and Jade.

## Building from source

You need JDK 21.

```
./gradlew build
```

The jar is written to `build/libs`. Other useful tasks are `runClient`, `runServer` and `runData`.
