**Kings & Monsters** is an adventure and progression mod built around organized monster factions: creatures with territory to defend, relics worth stealing, strongholds to conquer, and rulers powerful enough to enforce their claim.

I’m excited to bring you the **Ogre Tribe**, the first complete faction in Kings & Monsters. Raid its Outposts, earn its favor, trade with its least courageous businessman, follow Fort Maps into royal territory, and challenge the Ogre King for control of the bog’s buried iron. Version 2.0 rebuilds the Tribe with new animations, attacks and sounds, and adds a swamp full of Bog Toads, tameable Mudsnout Boars, new structures and a favor and quest system.

The Tribe has two moods toward you. **Anger** is shared by the whole Tribe and rises when you raid tribute or kill Ogres; hold **H** to see it. As it rises, patrols grow stronger and hunt the players responsible, but a group at one base is only hunted by one patrol per cooldown. Beat the patrols, buy an Ogre Truce, or give it time and the Tribe cools off.

**Favor is yours alone**, a personal reputation tracked for every player. Hurting Ogres costs you favor, while tribute and honest work earn it back. Build up enough and you are **appeased**: patrols stop hunting you, Ogres leave you alone, Captains talk to you without a coin, and the Ogre King will hear you out.

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1585531/description_c94a4b36-e8a6-4d56-8983-b7208f81d0e3.png)

**Gold Coin** — The Tribe’s tribute. Give coins to the King, a Captain or the Merchant for favor, or hold one out to an Ogre to pass unharmed for a while.

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1585531/description_0b8e21e9-86fb-4846-8e15-215dee9abe54.png)

**Golden Ogre Tooth** — Rarer and worth far more. Teeth buy the best stock and relics.

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1585531/description_4aefc5cd-9d9f-4ca5-b528-dca43a73e033.png)

**King’s Tribute Bundle** — A sealed bundle of tribute from the Merchant. It opens exactly once.

*   **Quests** — Captains hand out small errands, the King wants harder hunts, and he always keeps one big job among his offers: an Elder Guardian, the Warden, the Wither, Ravagers or Ghast tears. Quests pay favor, experience, coins and, for the hard ones, teeth.
*   **The price of a fight** — A stray hit costs a little favor and only the Ogre you struck fights back. Hurt that Ogre badly, though, and it fights you to the death. Every kill costs 5 favor, and a second kill within five minutes turns the whole Tribe against you.
*   **Your warband** — The Ogres you summon keep the peace while you are on good terms with the Tribe.

## The Ogre Merchant

A coward, a professional, and, when properly paid, a remarkable source of useful contraband. He wanders with an escort whose strength reflects tribal anger and stays about 40 minutes. Threaten him and the escort turns on you; trade with him for food, tools, relics, storage and rare Ogre goods.

Every Merchant carries one **Fort Map** and always offers the **Ogre Truce**, which trades a Golden Ogre Tooth to reduce the Tribe’s anger. Gold buys practical stock, diamonds buy better merchandise, and Golden Ogre Teeth open the most carefully guarded compartments of his pack.

## The Bog

The swamp has teeth now. **Bog Toads** are big, patient predators, and their young, the **Bog Poles**, hatch from eggs on the banks. Their bite carries **Bogrot**, and what the swamp gives up becomes some of the best gear in the mod. Open the list for the toads, the Bogfang Blade and the Boghide armor.

## The Plains

Ogre **Outposts** are tribal territory built around guarded tribute and one of three leaders: a Grunt Captain, Ogre Mage or Ogre Brute. Their treasure gives early Ogre materials, trophies and the first trail toward the King, but opening it counts as theft by the whole Tribe. A Plains Outpost reliably holds a **Fort Map** to the nearest Ogre Fort.

## The Ogre King’s Fort

Fort Maps lead to the **Ogre King’s Fort**: a fortified royal settlement of guards, tribute, trials, towers and workshops. Its **Royal Defence** calls elite Ogre encounters built around Grunt Captains, Mages and Brutes, and facing them first makes the King easier. On Forge 1.20.1 the trials use ordinary spawners and begin when you come close.

At the heart waits the Ogre King, a multi-stage boss fight with a new boss track by Soul. Defeating him completes **Usurped**, breaks his claim over the world’s Bog Iron and leaves equipment worthy of a new ruler. If the Tribe trusts you, you can also trade with him, ask for a fair duel, or take the crown and call warbands to fight at your side.
