# Kings & Monsters

**Kings & Monsters** is an adventure and progression mod about organized monster factions: creatures with territory to defend, relics worth stealing, strongholds to conquer, and rulers strong enough to enforce their claim.

Version 2.0 is the biggest update yet. The Ogre Tribe has been rebuilt with new animations, new attacks, new sounds and smoother fights, and the world around it has grown: a swamp full of Bog Toads, Mudsnout boars you can tame and ride, new structures, a favor and quest system, and a new boss track for the Ogre King.

## The Ogre Tribe

Ogres aren't lone monsters waiting to be picked off one at a time. They defend each other, hold fortified ground, rally around stronger leaders, and remember when their tribute gets stolen.

The whole Tribe shares one **anger level**. Raiding guarded tribute or killing ogres near their camps makes things worse, and the patrols that come after you get bigger and nastier the angrier they are. Beat the patrols, buy an Ogre Truce, or give it time and they cool off.

Every ogre has been animated again from scratch, with new attacks, new voices and new attack sounds. They also chase you in straight lines now instead of zigzagging, so fights feel a lot better.

## Earn the Tribe's Favor

Anger is the whole Tribe's mood. **Favor is yours alone**, a personal reputation tracked for every player. Hurting ogres costs you favor, and tribute and honest work earn it back.

Build up enough and you're **appeased**: patrols stop hunting you, ogres leave you alone, Captains talk to you without a coin, and the Ogre King will hear you out.

- **Gold Coins** are the Tribe's tribute token. Hold one out to an ogre to pass unharmed for a while, or hand them to the King, a Captain or the Ogre Merchant to raise your favor directly.
- **Golden Ogre Teeth** are rarer and worth far more. The Merchant's best stock and the King's own relics are priced in teeth.
- **Quests**: speak to a Captain or the King and pick from three jobs. Captains hand out smaller work, the King wants harder things, and the bold may be offered something greater. Quests pay favor, experience and usually a coin. Harder ones pay teeth.

*Everything is explained in more detail in the in-game compendium.*

## The Ogre King and his court

Reach the Ogre Fort and face the ruler of the Tribe in a multi-stage boss fight built on heavy melee attacks, ground slams you can jump over, summons and the strength of his people. The fight has a brand new boss track made by Soul.

Defeating him opens up royal equipment, powerful artifacts and the bog iron his rule kept locked away.

There's another way in for those the Tribe trusts. With enough favor you can trade with the King for his relics, ask for a **fair duel** where he fights to a yield and not to the death, or take the crown and call warbands of ogres to fight at your side.

---

## Mudsnout boars

Giant clay-grey boars roam the plains in small packs. Wild ones keep their distance and give you a warning snort if you crowd them. Leave them alone and they leave you alone, but cross them and the whole pack answers.

Win one over with food and you can **tame it, saddle it and ride it**, charging through anything in your way. Breed them for speed and health to raise a real war boar. Some ogres ride them too, and their hide makes **Blackhide armor**.

---

## The swamp

The swamp has teeth now.

- **Bog Toads** are big, patient predators that hop and bite with a rot that ogres have learned to fear.
- **Bog Poles** are their young. They hatch from eggs on the banks and grow into toads in time.
- **Bogrot** is a slow, armor-ignoring rot that hits ogres especially hard. A toad-killed Ogre Grunt can rise again as a **Plague Ogre**.
- **Bogfang Blades**, **Boghide armor**, potions and toad cooking come from what the swamp gives up.

---

## Explore and conquer

Find ogre structures across the plains and swamps: guarded outposts, supply and moving camps, abandoned camps, the King's quarry, Trapper huts and boar traps, mud wallows and the enormous Ogre Fort.

These places hold guarded resources, progression items, merchants, maps and clues that lead deeper into Ogre territory.

### Ogre Grunt

The ordinary muscle of the Tribe. Grunts are aggressive, stubborn and rarely far from their kin. They rush enemies head on and may leave a victim Crippled with a vicious bite.

![Ogre Archer](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1585531/description_7a906b50-dda4-4224-9ee7-6d23bd1cfb7e.png)

A sharp-eyed hunter who supports the Tribe from a distance. Archers keep enemies in bow range, reposition when threatened and cover their stronger companions. Some ride Mudsnout boars.

### Ogre Mage
![Ogre Mage](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1585531/description_ecb4705b-09d4-4db0-973f-bf0e5aa7bb7d.png)

One of the King's captains, who leads through strange magic instead of brute strength. Mages control the battlefield with poison fog and a line of jaws that snaps up out of the mire, while ordinary Grunts keep you busy.

### Ogre Brute
![Ogre Brute](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1585531/description_6dfc7656-531e-44db-a8bf-5ad2627b3c0a.png)

One of the King's captains, raised up for sheer size and strength. Brutes lead through fear, soak up punishment and crush anyone reckless enough to fight them up close.

### Grunt Captain
![Grunt Captain](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1585531/description_da96d075-6f2e-4454-be72-c9fa924c602b.png)

One of the King's captains, chosen for experience and raw determination. Captains lead smaller patrols and outposts from the front, and they're the ones who give out errands.

### Ogre Guard
![Ogre Guard](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1585531/description_5ab5c8dd-809b-4f97-a882-2adfb566c2ec.png)

A tiny but heavily armed defender posted at the Tribe's most important ground. What Guards lack in size they make up for in discipline, stubbornness and an almost comical refusal to leave their post. They carry Hunter's Spears for melee and throwing.

### Ogre Trapper
A hunter ogre found at Trapper huts and boar traps. He throws Bogjaw Traps to pin you in place and cuts with his Trapper's Knife.

### Plague Ogre
What's left of an Ogre Grunt after the mire's rot finishes with it. Plague Ogres wander swamps and the edges of plains outposts, immune to the rot they carry, and the rest of the Tribe hunts them on sight.

### Ogre Merchant
![Ogre Merchant](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1585531/description_3dc8b1dd-7d9f-4a40-8f2f-3bfa80810b70.png)

The least courageous member of the Tribe, and maybe its cleverest. The Merchant would rather bargain than fight, selling unusual supplies, relics, Fort Maps and even temporary peace with the Tribe.

### The Ogre King
![The Ogre King](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1585531/description_22e98795-98e0-4140-9cf5-613fb1a9c19f.png)

The ruler of the Tribe and the authority behind every outpost, patrol and guarded vein of Bog Iron. He commands from his Fort, surrounded by warriors who still recognize his claim. Beating him in battle, or in a fair duel, breaks his hold over the bog's buried iron.

## Armor

<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>

### Bog Iron Armor
With the full set, a hard enough hit grants Unyielding for 10 seconds: +4 armor, +2 toughness, 30% knockback resistance, +3 attack damage and a shockwave that pushes back everything within 4 blocks. 30-second cooldown.

### Boghide Armor
Made from smelted Bog Toad Skin. The boots give faster swimming and double air in water, and a toad leap on land. The full set makes you immune to Bogrot and shortens the leap's cooldown.

### Blackhide Armor
Made from boar hide, with two Mudsnout Tusks on the helmet. Each piece has a 5% chance to turn an arrow aside. The full set builds momentum while you sprint into a Charge that rams enemies.

### Brute's Helm
Heavy iron taken from a Brute. On top of its armor it adds to your maximum health.

### Bogsteel Chestplate
Bog iron purified by ogres. Its chest defense beats netherite, and now and then a blow glances off and does no damage at all.

### Ogre Loincloth
Leggings-slot armor with 1 armor, 0.5 toughness and 15% knockback resistance. Worn, it gives you Strength I and Resistance I.

</details>

## Enchantments

<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>

### Tyrant
Damage rises with every hostile near you, up to +1.6, +2.75 and +3.75 at levels I to III. Works on swords, axes, maces and the ogre relics, but not tridents or spears.

### Heavy Throw
Thrown weapons hit harder and knock back further, but fly slower. Works on tridents and the Hunter's Spear.

### Barbed
Hunter's Spear only. Barbed I makes its wound Bleeding, and Barbed II makes it Greater Bleeding.

### Bogburst
When a Bogrot infection ruptures, the rot spreads to nearby hostiles: 5 blocks for 15 seconds at level I, 7 blocks for 25 seconds at level II.

### Timber
Chop one log and every log connected to it comes down, up to 128. Crouch to take just one.

### Brightwick
Candle Helmet only. Adds one level of light per level, up to three.

</details>

## Effects

<details>
<summary>Spoiler</summary>

### Bogrot
Slows movement and attack speed by 10% and deals damage that ignores armor, for 45 seconds. Ogres take 15% more from it and the Ogre King 50% more. Milk cures it.

### Bleeding
Comes in Lesser, normal and Greater. Deals periodic damage, drains hunger and cuts healing. Creatures with no blood can't bleed.

### Dazed
Reduces movement speed by 20% and attack speed by 15%. Inflicted by the heaviest ogre attacks.

### Crippled
Reduces movement speed by 15% and stops sprinting. Ogre Grunt bites and thrown Hunter's Spears can inflict it.

### Hamstrung
A cut tendon: the legs can no longer push off the ground.

### Bogfume Rage
Ogres standing in an Ogre Mage's poison fog deal 20% more damage and take 15% less.

### Ogre's Bane
From Toad Stew. +15% damage against ogres for 5 minutes.

### Bogstride
Worn in water with Boghide Boots: faster swimming and a longer breath.

### Charge
From the full Blackhide set at top speed. Your next ram or hit flings its target away.

</details>

## Installation and dependencies

### Required

- **Minecraft 1.21.1**
- **NeoForge**
- **Java 21**
- **GeckoLib**
- **Curios API**

Install Kings & Monsters and its required dependencies on both the client and the server.

### Optional compatibility

- **Patchouli**: in-game compendium
- **Just Enough Items (JEI)**: recipes and effect information
- **Jade**: tooltips for Ogre mechanics
- **AppleSkin**: food information for Ogre meals
- **Better Combat**: club attack animations
- **Boss Checklist**: lists the Ogre King
- **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks**: ogres resist spell damage according to their role
- **RPG Series** (Wizards, Paladins & Priests, Rogues & Warriors, Archers, via Spell Power): ogres resist their spell damage as well
- **Wildscapes**: structure spacing, so Ogre structures and Wildscapes structures don't crowd each other
